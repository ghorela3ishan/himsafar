Are humans really the most intelligent species ?

Introduction

It’s a widely considered notion amongst humans that we are the most intelligent species on this planet. If someones goes to the extent of validating and question the reasoning, many of us would come up with achievements like humans are the only species that came up with the idea of language, society, harnessing resources on the planet, technological innovations etc.

The Notion

If we pursue the notion, it does make sense. Let’s think if there are there any other species on this planet who have been able to built trains or communication technology ? Did any species learn how to cook their food ? Did they try to understand what lies beyond our planet ? Did they ever try to figure out what’s inside them ? Did they pursue spirituality ?
