Yes! As much as dead it can be. Do you really think you are using those favorite social media, communication or shopping  apps? Think again! Because it’s not you who is using them, it’s them using you. 

Okay it might feel like just another stupid rant until you see those water and energy hungry computational machines and influence and capital hungry owners behind those little demonic terminals in your hands and everybody else’s hands all around you. Yes I am talking about smart phones. 

Let me remind you of the [boiling frog strategy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiling_frog) ( *a metaphor for a situation where a threat is so gradual that it goes unnoticed until it's too late to react* ). In retrospect do you realise if there was ever an inflection point where you got hooked onto the doomless scrolling or subconsciously closing the app to only open it again ? No, right ? That’s because you were tricked into the pit but gradually that you didn’t even realise. One may argue,  it looks harmless, helps kill the boredom, gives you an opportunity to join the pop culture with everybody else so why criticise such an empowering innovation ?

**The crux of every human and technology interaction at this point of time is to not to aid the consciousness but to captivate it.** It’s a tussle between human minds and highly efficient algorithms powered by giant computation capabilities and insanely large chunks of capital and everybody is swept in this wave to tussle, right from a toddler to the eldest member of the family draining away their cognitive capabilities evolved over generations of evolution. Most of us who don’t understand how this technology works would hardly ever know that the one single phone they use is way much more powerful than all of their conscious minds combined and hence they end us consuming and believing everything they are fed

What happens once someone accidentally falls into a pit ? They try to get out as soon as possible! That’s what seems reasonable too. But what if they don’t realise they are in a pit ? What if they feel comfortable ? They continue to remain trapped. 

**What if the pit is actually an economic opportunity ?** In a free market, OF COURSE there will be a bunch of magicians who will try to sell a solution ( like an air purifier to a city that’s choking in smoke ) and gradually the pit will be turned into a product. 

I am sorry to break this to you but the internet is already the pit you didn’t realise you fell into. It’s definitely not that happy place where you used to meet your friends virtually after school,college or office. It’s definitely not the place where you find real factual information of something happening on a different continent. Now it is just a crowded place where everybody is trying to sell you something and if not guess what ? You are just being monitored every second you are using that piece of technology so that you can be profiled, cloned into a virtual identity to predict what’s your next move or worst decide your next move and voila, trick you into buying something. 

The worst part is every time it’s going to be sold as an innovative wrench to solve the crisis only to realise later that you eventually didn’t buy the solution, you bought the crisis. 

The latest LLM advancements with a failed attempt to replicate consciousness is yet another example. They sold the dream of [AGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_general_intelligence) with machines finally taking us into the Artificial Super Intelligence Era solving the mysteries of cosmos and ended up filling the internet with filthy, crappy machines generating sloppy content irreversibly ruining the fabric of virtual society.
